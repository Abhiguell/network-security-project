🎣 Gotcha! That was a (simulated) phishing message

Don't worry: nothing was collected. This page has no form and asks for nothing. But in a real attack, the next page would have looked exactly like our sign-in page and stolen your password.

Why you received it

The CyberSim Lab just ran a spyware simulation that exposed (synthetic) customer names, emails and phone numbers. Criminals who steal this kind of data immediately send convincing "verify your account" messages to the victims, because they already know your name and that you shop here.

Red flags in the message

  • Urgency and threats: "within 24 hours", "or your account will be suspended".
  • Unexpected request: you didn't ask to verify anything.
  • A link instead of a normal sign-in: real stores ask you to log in the usual way.
  • Asks for secrets: passwords, OTP codes or card numbers. LabMart (and any real shop) never asks for these.
  • Timing: it arrives right after news of a data leak.

What to do instead

  • Don't click. Open the shop yourself by typing its address, or use the official app.
  • Check the real address bar: look-alike domains (labmart-verify.co) are a giveaway.
  • Never share an OTP code with anyone, not even "customer service".
  • Turn on two-factor authentication and use a unique password per site.
  • Report suspicious messages to the store.

What the store should do

  • Notify affected customers quickly and honestly (required by UU PDP).
  • Warn customers that it will never ask for passwords or OTPs by message.
  • Force password resets and offer MFA.
