What happened

  • A hidden component read the customer database through a trusted channel.
  • Personal data was exposed: names, emails, phones, addresses and purchase history.
  • The store flagged it as Spyware Data Exposure + Mass Data Access and opened an incident.
  • Follow-up phishing: shoppers on LabMart now get a fake "verify your account" pop-up. Clicks are logged as Phishing Lure Clicked. It disappears on reset.

Real-world consequences

  • Phishing and scam calls targeting customers ("your order has a problem…").
  • Legal duty to notify under Indonesia's UU PDP (Law 27/2022); possible fines.
  • Loss of customer trust and reputation.

Defences shown / recommended

  • Least privilege: the API can only see synthetic rows.
  • Passwords stored as salted hashes; cards handled by Midtrans (never stored).
  • Alert on bulk PII reads (DLP), signed API calls, audit trail of the operator.
  • Encrypt sensitive columns; MFA for admin accounts.
