What happened

  • A malicious payload ran on the store server and "encrypted" its files.
  • Product backups, invoices and reports became unusable; a ransom note was dropped.
  • The store detected mass file access + mass rename and opened a CRITICAL incident.

Real-world consequences

  • Store offline: no sales during recovery (hours to weeks).
  • Double extortion: attackers threaten to leak stolen data.
  • Paying does not guarantee recovery and funds criminals.

Defences

  • 3-2-1 offline backups with regular restore tests (shown by Reset).
  • EDR behaviour detection, least-privilege file permissions.
  • Patch management, email filtering, network segmentation.
  • An incident response plan: isolate → eradicate → restore.
